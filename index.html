Virtual Assistant

Remote · Part-Time / Full-Time

About the Role

MoveOn is hiring a Virtual Assistant to support cross-border trade operations remotely. The role covers day-to-day data entry, scheduling, communication and research tasks, with flexible working hours chosen by the candidate within a 24-hour window.

Job Type & Working Hours

Full-Time: 8 hours per day

Part-Time: 4 hours per day

Salary

Full-Time: 30,000 – 35,000 BDT / month

Part-Time: 15,000 – 17,500 BDT / month (half of full-time)

Requirements

  • Fluent in English

Skills

  • Data entry
  • Scheduling
  • Communication
  • Research

Experience

Both freshers and experienced candidates are encouraged to apply. Selection preference is given to candidates with relevant experience.

Freshers can apply — prior experience is a plus, not a requirement.

Responsibilities

  • Handle day-to-day data entry accurately and on schedule.
  • Manage scheduling and calendar coordination tasks.
  • Communicate with team members and stakeholders in English.
  • Carry out research tasks as assigned to support operations.
